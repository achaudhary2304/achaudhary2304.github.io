PaperLens helps readers ask passage-specific questions and follow up on research papers.

## Specialized agents

Built with LangGraph and LangChain, the companion routes questions to agents for paper context, concept explanations, mathematical reasoning, and related research. Three MCP servers support document retrieval, academic search, and mathematical tools.

## Retrieval and source-linked answers

BM25-based retrieval-augmented generation finds relevant passages within individual papers and across multi-paper projects. Explanations link back to supporting source passages.

Overlapping text chunks, persistent paper indexes, content-hash caching, and parallel research execution reduce repeated processing.
