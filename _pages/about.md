I am a Computer Science undergraduate at BITS Pilani, interested in deep learning, computer vision, and robotics. My work spans neural eigensolvers, vision pipelines, and autonomous rover navigation.

## Internships

### IBM Research · Research Intern
**Algorithms and Applications Team · Bangalore, India · May – August 2026**

- Developed a neural eigensolver that uses coarse spectral estimates and substantially fewer sample points than full tensor-grid methods for high-dimensional eigenvalue problems.
- Built a Transformer-based probe-response encoder to predict the first few eigenvalues from operator actions, reducing reliance on expensive grid-based solves.
- Improved and evaluated solver stability using spectral shifts, delayed target updates, and penalty-based eigenvalue selection on benchmarks up to 20 dimensions.
- This work was accepted at the **NeurIPS Sim2Science Workshop**.
- Contributed to a separate invention on spectrally safe workload scheduling to mitigate probing-based attacks on servers, selected for patent filing.

### Anitum · Computer Vision Intern
**Remote · May – July 2025**

- Built a PyTorch vision pipeline with a DINOv2 backbone for depth estimation and semantic segmentation in a single forward pass.
- Optimized data cleaning, augmentation, and training for YOLO-based person detection, improving the previous base model’s performance by **10%**.
- Combined PoseC3D with person detection to recognize actions of specific people in crowded environments.
- Explored XFeat for face matching.
