I am a Computer Science undergraduate at BITS Pilani, interested in deep learning, computer vision, and robotics. My work spans neural eigensolvers, vision pipelines, and autonomous rover navigation.

## Internships

### IBM Research · Research Intern
**Algorithms and Applications Team · Bangalore, India · May – August 2026**

Worked on neural eigensolvers and Transformer models for high-dimensional eigenvalue problems. Explored ways to make the solvers more efficient and stable, with the work accepted at the NeurIPS Sim2Science Workshop. Also helped file a patent!

### Anitum · Computer Vision Intern
**Remote · May – July 2025**

Worked on computer vision pipelines for depth estimation, segmentation, and person detection. Combined detection with action recognition to track what specific people were doing in crowded scenes.

## Selected Projects

I mostly make projects that I use myself. I was reading a lot of papers and kept switching to ChatGPT for small doubts, so I made [PaperReader](https://github.com/achaudhary2304/paperreader). It makes it easier to ask questions, annotate, and organize my papers and slides. The alternatives I found were either paid or missing features I wanted, so I made my own.
